About Me

📸 Add action photo here

With over 10 years of experience as both a Personal Trainer and Instructor Trainer, I have dedicated my career to helping individuals achieve their fitness goals through consistency, science-backed training, and personalised coaching.

Holding a Bachelor Degree in Sports & Exercise Science from Edith Cowan University, my approach combines academic knowledge with real-world results — from weight loss and muscle building to rehabilitation and mobility.

Recognised as a Top 3 Personal Trainer at Fitness First Singapore in 2024, I bring the same dedication and drive to every client I work with, regardless of their starting point.
